# Sunken Reach palette card

Sunken Reach is a small, shallow lagoon that belongs to Skywake's weathered
coastal family while using the submerged equivalent of its materials. The sand
is pale and softly rippled under turquoise water. Salmon and muted-gold coral
are balanced by sea-green kelp, so the reef reads warmly against the cool
water rather than becoming one blue mass.

The wreck is deliberately a different neighbour: dark waterlogged timber,
near-black seams, oxidised verdigris fittings, and restrained amber lanterns.
Its boards use the same hand-built, worn construction language as the island's
piers and sheds, but time under water has darkened and softened them. Coral and
kelp can claim the hull without repainting it into a reef-coloured building.

Light comes in sparse pale shafts from the surface, small drifting bubbles, and
a legible amber chest/current accent. The shafts and current are navigation
signs, not a blanket of glow: open water stays readable above and below the
wreck beams.
