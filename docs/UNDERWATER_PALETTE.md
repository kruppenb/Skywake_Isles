# Sunken Reach palette card

Sunken Reach is a large, submerged expedition realm. Its common construction
language is hand-laid, eroded ocean material: pitted stone, waterlogged planks,
soft silt, and growth that accumulates in seams and along current-facing edges.
It is deliberately unlike the island's wall-and-roof rows in the Area identity
contract: no roof system is used, and ruins vary their stone, metal and growth
instead of repeating an on-land building treatment. The original wreck remains
the near-shore timber neighbour, with dark waterlogged boards, near-black seams
and oxidised fittings; it is not recoloured to impersonate the reef.

The contract check is intentional: Sunken Reach shares the island's handmade,
weathered construction language, but no landmark copies a shipped wall/roof
pair. Adjacent landmarks also diverge: the wreck is plank-built, the bells are
pale ashlar with bronze frames, the vents are dry-looking basalt columns, and
the graveyard combines broken timber with green reefstone. Light is navigational
instead of decorative: sparse surface shafts, tall return beacon, calm treasure
glows and warm vent seams establish direction without turning the water into a
single luminous blue mass.

## Art pass material and structure checks

The art pass keeps the shared hand-built language while giving each region a
different structural vocabulary. Surface materials should read at swimmer
height: shell grains and shallow ripples in Sunken Reach; broad overlapping
coral shelves with branching colonies in Coral Gardens; ropey root holdfasts,
olive silt pockets, and spaced jade fronds in Kelp Hollows; individually
coursed ashlar with recessed joints, bronze bell throats, and verdigris straps
in Bell Sanctuary; porous basalt with fractured lips, dark mineral seams, and
small amber vent mouths in Ember Vents; and separate collapsed hull frames,
keels, ribs, masts, copper fasteners, and reefstone in Crown Graveyard.

Before accepting a rendered view, check that the focal structure has visible
surface variation at gameplay distance, a readable route around or through it,
and a silhouette that differs from its neighboring landmark. The entry wreck
must remain plank-and-rib construction, the Coral Gardens spire organic, the
Bell Sanctuary towers ashlar and bronze, the Ember Vents chimney porous basalt,
and the Crown Graveyard hulls visibly distinct from both the wreck and the
stone landmarks. This verifies the Area identity contract: shared construction
and weathering quality, with no single reef-wide colour scheme.
