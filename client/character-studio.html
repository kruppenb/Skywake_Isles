Original prototype · not the live player model

A rough visual draft, shown for shape and surface review only. It is not accepted production quality and it is not wired into gameplay: the live player is still the procedural model in client/models.js. Known artefacts in this build include shoulder seam tabs, solid facial hair and coat-skirt penetration in a deep crouch.

Lighting

Island lighting copies the game's light values (ACES, exposure 1.18, hemisphere #d9f6ff/#779e7a 2.2, sun #fff0d0 2.7 at −70, 140, 80, sky #85d9ee) on a plain ground disc — not the island itself.

Animation

Clips drive the prototype only; the current player holds its neutral ground stance with the flintlock drawn. Playback starts paused when the system asks for reduced motion.

Crew accent

Tints only the prototype's crew_accent material and rebuilds the current player in the same colour. The five crew colours mirror the in-game list.

Surface

Honest limits

  • Rough visual prototype, not accepted production quality.
  • Known artefacts: shoulder seam tabs, solid facial hair, coat-skirt penetration in a deep crouch.
  • No gameplay integration: no weapons, no glider, no session, no player swap.
  • Walk is a rig proof, not final locomotion.
  • Bounds are measured in the rest pose, before a clip is applied.
