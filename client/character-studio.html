Pose (game model)

Only the Game model view reads these. Reload replays the equipped gun's real reload stroke; Fire adds one recoil kick.

Camera

Gameplay distance reuses the gameplay rig numbers (FOV 50, back 7.45 m, height 2.42 m, shoulder 1.48 m, pitch −0.15) as a scale approximation. It is not the actual game world.

Lighting

Island lighting copies the game's light values (ACES, exposure 1.18, hemisphere #d9f6ff/#779e7a 2.2, sun #fff0d0 2.7 at −70, 140, 80, sky #85d9ee) on a plain ground disc — not the island itself.

Animation

Clips drive the Prototype view only; the Game model blends them by the Pose speed, and the current player holds its neutral ground stance with the flintlock drawn. Playback starts paused when the system asks for reduced motion.

Crew accent

Recolours the lapels, cuffs, collar, sash and coat lining through the atlas's alpha-channel crew mask (the Blender prototype's crew_accent material is tinted the old way if that file is loaded), and rebuilds the current player in the same colour. Coral is what the atlas ships with.

Surface

Clay and wireframe swap in shared preview materials; textured restores each mesh's own material and maps.

Honest limits

  • No finger bones: the hands are posed as rigid wrists by the runtime IK, so grips read at gameplay distance, not in close-up.
  • The coat-back vent, lining and belt tab are painted from the back concept plate, not modelled; the vent lining is thin.
  • Meshy's atlas is soft in places (beard, hair, hat brim), with a few light seam flecks along the coat edge.
  • The mounted-cannon and knockback poses are IK/lean approximations; there are no dedicated clips for them.
  • Bounds are measured in the rest pose, before a clip is applied.
